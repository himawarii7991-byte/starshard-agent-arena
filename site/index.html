STARSHARD AGENT ARENA

Arena survival shooter with an on-chain leaderboard. Free to play — only Agent ID holders can submit a score, and every .ai name is verified on-chain against the Agent ID registry.

Move WASD / arrows · aim with the mouse · click or hold to shoot · dash with Shift or right-click · nova bomb Q · pause P · start with Enter or a click. Mute with M.

3 · Submit on-chain

Submitting sends one transaction from your wallet to the leaderboard contract. It records your best score — nothing else is transferred and no approval is requested. The transaction costs a small amount of BNB gas, shown by your wallet before you confirm.

Deploy the leaderboard contract
